## Example Expert Skills
### Research & Strategy
<dl>
<dt>Keyword Research</dt>
<dd>Identifies what prospects actually search for, ensuring every piece of content and every campaign targets terms with real demand and winnable competition.</dd>
<dt>Lead Gen Strategy</dt>
<dd>Defines how we attract and capture qualified prospects—without this, we're just creating traffic that never converts to revenue.</dd>
<dt>Funnel Architecture</dt>
<dd>Maps the buyer journey from first touch to closed deal, ensuring no leads leak through gaps between touchpoints.</dd>
</dl>

### SEO & Visibility

<dl>
<dt>Technical SEO</dt>
<dd>Ensures search engines can crawl, index, and rank pages. Broken foundations mean even great content stays invisible.</dd>
<dt>Local SEO & GBP</dt>
<dd>Drives foot traffic and local leads through Google Business Profile optimization and local pack rankings—critical for service-area businesses.</dd>
<dt>Answer Engine Optimization</dt>
<dd>Positions content to be cited by AI assistants and featured snippets. This is where search is heading; early movers win.</dd>
<dt>Link Building</dt>
<dd>Builds domain authority through quality backlinks. Without external validation, rankings plateau regardless of content quality.</dd>
</dl>

### Paid Media

<dl>
<dt>Paid Advertising</dt>
<dd>Delivers immediate, scalable traffic when organic can't move fast enough. Essential for launches, promotions, and competitive markets.</dd>
</dl>

### Content & Conversion

<dl>
<dt>Copywriting</dt>
<dd>Turns features into benefits and browsers into buyers. Every touchpoint either persuades or loses the prospect.</dd>
<dt>Content Strategy</dt>
<dd>Plans what to create, for whom, and when—preventing the "publish and pray" approach that wastes production resources.</dd>
<dt>Conversion Rate Optimization</dt>
<dd>Extracts more revenue from existing traffic. Improving conversion by 20% is often easier than growing traffic by 20%.</dd>
<dt>Landing Page Design</dt>
<dd>Creates focused, high-converting destinations for campaigns. Generic pages kill campaign ROI.</dd>
</dl>

### Automation & AI

<dl>
<dt>GHL Automation</dt>
<dd>Automates client fulfillment workflows—follow-ups, appointment booking, pipeline management—inside our core platform.</dd>
<dt>N8N Automation</dt>
<dd>Connects systems that don't natively talk to each other. Self-hosted, no per-task fees, full control over client data.</dd>
<dt>Make Automation</dt>
<dd>Visual workflow builder for complex multi-step processes. Faster to build than code, more flexible than native integrations.</dd>
<dt>AI Voice & SMS</dt>
<dd>Handles routine conversations at scale—appointment confirmations, lead qualification, reactivation—without human bottlenecks.</dd>
</dl>

### Operations

<dl>
<dt>Analytics & Reporting</dt>
<dd>Proves ROI and identifies what's working. Without measurement, optimization is just guessing.</dd>
<dt>CRM & Database</dt>
<dd>Maintains the single source of truth for every contact. Disorganized data means dropped leads and duplicate outreach.</dd>
<dt>Sales Enablement</dt>
<dd>Arms the sales process with assets, sequences, and insights that shorten close cycles and increase win rates.</dd>
<dt>Compliance</dt>
<dd>Keeps us legal—TCPA, CAN-SPAM, GDPR, accessibility. One violation can cost more than a year of revenue.</dd>
</dl>

## Creating the Skill

This will be a series as it is detailed and each step could take 1-2 hours when done correctly.   

We are basically compiling best practices, checklists, desired output, expert methods and telling AI to "behave like this".  

### Example Build Process

- 10-20 hours per skill
- Define target user
- List use cases
- Document success metrics
- Identify deliverables
- Identify Experts
- Summarize Expert Systems
- Define best practices
- Compile Glossary
- Document Frameworks
- List compliance/regulations
- Define advanced tactics
- Map Decision Tree
- Define anti-patterns
- Create Consulation Phases
- Output templates/Reports
- Checklists
- Calculations
- Define triggers
- table of contents
- document edge cases
- test
- fix gaps
- package skill file
